Hospital-grade tools, made home-ready

Recovering at home often needs more than just people — it needs reliable, sanitized, and easy-to-use medical equipment. From hospital beds to oxygen concentrators, we help you rent or buy high-quality tools delivered and installed quickly.

Our team provides setup, training, and support — so you can focus on care, not complications.

What makes it work

We partner only with certified suppliers and ensure full sanitization, maintenance, and servicing protocols.

  • Hospital beds (manual & motorized)

  • Oxygen concentrators and cylinders

  • BiPAP and CPAP machines

  • Wheelchairs, walkers, commodes

  • Suction machines and monitors

  • Air mattresses (anti-bedsore)

  • Nebulizers and pulse oximeters

  • Short-term rental and long-term purchase options

  • Emergency same-day delivery in Patna & Bihar

Care is complete only when equipment and people work seamlessly — and we make sure they do.
